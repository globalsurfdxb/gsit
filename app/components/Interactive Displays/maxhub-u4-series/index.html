Features

Ready for Lessons & Everyday Group Discussions

The U4 Series pairs an Android platform with an AI camera and an 8-mic array for teaching and group discussions.

Octa-Core Platform

An 8nm octa-core processor works with a 6 TOPS NPU, 16GB RAM and 256GB ROM.

AI Camera

The 50MP camera supports auto framing, speaker tracking, person counting and random election.

8-Mic Array

Voices are picked up across a 180° angle from as far as 12m.

100W USB-C

Rear USB-C delivers up to 100W PD, and the front port supplies up to 65W.

Eye-Care Certified

The screen is TÜV certified as Flicker Free and Low Blue Light.

Think&Ink Writing

Palm rejection, sharp erase and erasing while writing are part of the touch experience.

Overview

A Bright Panel With Flexible Connections

The U4 Series runs MAXHUB EDU OS V4 on Android 15 with Google EDLA certification, with Google Play, Google Drive, Google Meet, YouTube and other apps available. The 4K screen reaches 500 nits and has ambient light and colour temperature sensors.

  • Four HDMI inputs and a DisplayPort input are available for wired sources.
  • A TF card slot expands storage by up to 1TB.
  • The design is rated IP5X for dust resistance.
  • Energy Star certification applies to all three sizes.
  • An OPS slot accepts the optional OPS62A or OPS72A modules.
Built-In Software

MAXHUB Tools & Google Apps Included

MAXHUB software covers whiteboarding, screen sharing and device management, and Google apps add more options.

MAXHUB Class Pro

Class Pro is the whiteboard tool on the panel.

MAXHUB Connect

MAXHUB Connect sends device screens to the panel.

MAXHUB Pivot

IT staff can manage panels through MAXHUB Pivot.

Google Apps

Google Play, Drive, Meet, Maps and YouTube are available.

Other Built-In Apps

A browser, a cloud drive, a note app and a palette are also provided.

FAQs

Common Questions on U4 Series

01Which sizes does the U4 Series include?

Three: the U6540 (65″), the U7540 (75″) and the U8640 (86″). Each has a 4K screen with 500 nits maximum brightness.

02Does it need a separate computer?

Android 15 with Google EDLA certification is built in, with 16GB RAM and 256GB storage on board. An OPS slot is also available for the optional OPS62A or OPS72A modules.

03Which Google apps can be used?

With Google EDLA certification, the panel offers Google Play, Google Drive, Google Meet, Google Maps and YouTube, among others.

04How many wired sources can it take?

There are four HDMI inputs, a DisplayPort input and two USB-C ports, along with four USB 3.0 ports. The rear USB-C provides up to 100W PD and the front up to 65W.

05What can the camera do?

The single 50MP camera covers 115° diagonally and offers manual mode, auto framing, speaker tracking, person counting and random election.

06How is the U4 Series mounted?

The U4 Series does not use VESA mounting. Each panel includes a WIB9060K wall mount bracket and two display brackets, uses M7 × 56mm screws and sits 122mm from the wall.

07What comes with each panel?

The box holds a remote control, two SW13F styluses, power and HDMI cables (3m each), two display brackets, a WIB9060K wall mount bracket and an SI07B Wi-Fi module.

08Is the U4 Series protected against dust?

Yes. The panel is rated IP5X for dust resistance.
