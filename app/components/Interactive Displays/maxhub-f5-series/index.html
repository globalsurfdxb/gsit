Features

Clear to See, Easy to Hear & Simple to Use

The F5 Series gives teachers tools to present, write and include remote students in the same lesson.

Eye-Care Display

TÜV certification for low blue light and flicker-free viewing applies to both models.

Five-Lens Camera

Panorama, telephoto and fusion lenses combine to form a 60MP camera system.

SkyWave 2.2 Sound

Four speakers deliver 2 × 8W + 2 × 18W of output, with up to 2 × 12W + 2 × 25W at maximum.

8-Mic Pickup

An eight-microphone array covers 180° and picks up voices up to 12m away.

Think&Ink Writing

Direct annotation, multi-level object recognition and single pen dual colour are supported.

Windows 11 Pro PC

A 13th Gen Intel Core i5 module with 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage runs the system.

Overview

F5 Series for In-Room & Hybrid Lessons

MAXHUB EDU OS for Windows runs on the MT82S PC module, so teachers can use familiar Windows teaching tools. Remote and hybrid lessons are supported by the wide-angle camera system and the 8-mic array.

  • Side viewing keeps at least one third of the centre brightness at ±60°.
  • Colour accuracy is rated at Delta E ≤ 1, with 98% sRGB coverage.
  • HDMI 1 supports ARC, and both HDMI inputs carry 4K at 60Hz.
  • One USB-C connection handles video, audio, touch and LAN sharing.
  • Both models carry Energy Star certification.
Camera System

Panoramic & Close-Up Shots

The camera system pairs wide and telephoto lenses to show both the full classroom and closer detail.

Panorama Lens

One 8MP lens covers 145° diagonally and 128° horizontally.

Telephoto Lenses

Two 13MP lenses cover 57° diagonally for closer views.

Fusion Cameras

A pair of 13MP fusion cameras reaches 151° diagonally.

Photo & Video Capture

Photos reach 52MP, and video records up to 8K at 10fps.

Classroom Features

The camera also offers person counting, random election and gesture recognition.

FAQs

Common Questions on F5 Series

01Which models are in the F5 Series?

There are two: the F7550 with a 75″ screen and the F8650 with an 86″ screen. Both use a 4K wide viewing angle panel rated at 400 nits typical and 450 nits maximum.

02What powers the F5 Series?

The F5 runs Windows 11 Pro on the MT82S PC module, with a 13th Gen Intel Core i5, 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage. We can confirm the module configuration for your project.

03How does the camera help with hybrid lessons?

Five lenses cover the room: a panorama lens for the wide view, two telephoto lenses for closer detail and two fusion cameras. Person counting, random election and gesture recognition are also available.

04Which eye-care certifications does the screen have?

Both models hold TÜV Flicker Free and TÜV Low Blue Light certification, and the anti-glare surface has a 25% haze finish.

05How powerful are the speakers?

SkyWave 2.2 sound delivers 2 × 8W + 2 × 18W of output, rising to 2 × 12W + 2 × 25W at maximum, with 88 dB measured at 1m.

06What does the USB-C port carry?

Video, audio, touch and LAN network sharing all pass through the single USB-C port on the display.

07What is supplied in the box?

Each display ships with a 3m HDMI cable, two SW30 styluses, two wall mount strips and a WIB9060H wall mount plate. Please confirm the full box contents, including the power cord, when you enquire.

08How is the F5 mounted?

With the WIB9060H plate, the depth is 123.6mm on the F7550 and 125.8mm on the F8650. VESA patterns are 800 × 400mm and 800 × 600mm respectively, using M8 × 25mm screws.
