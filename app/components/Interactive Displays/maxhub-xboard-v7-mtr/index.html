Features

See, Hear & Share With Everyone Taking Part

The XBoard V7 MTR brings video, audio and on-screen work together for people in the room and those joining remotely.

Three 50MP Cameras

A wide camera with a 99° diagonal view works with two telephoto cameras that give 5x hybrid zoom.

AI Framing & Tracking

Auto framing, speaker tracking, multi-speaker tracking and intelligent focus adjust each shot.

16-Mic Array Pickup

The array picks up voices across 180°, with a best distance of 8m and a maximum of 15m.

Echo & Noise Control

Echo cancellation, AI noise cancellation and an intelligent audio fence process the room’s audio.

BYOD Laptop Mode

Once enabled, the USB-B touch port lets a laptop use the display’s cameras, microphones and touch.

50-Point Touch Writing

Two passive styluses are included, and the infrared touch layer reads up to 50 points.

Overview

Your Meeting Room in One Frame

Teams Rooms runs on a slot-in PC with Windows 11 IoT, so the display needs no separate computer. Content can be shared over HDMI at up to 4K on the room display, or wirelessly with a MAXHUB dongle or the MAXHUB Share app.

  • Colour accuracy stays within Delta E 1.5, and the panel covers 90% of the NTSC colour gamut.
  • Zero-parallax, 9H tempered glass has a 25% haze surface that reduces glare.
  • Ambient light and motion sensors are built into the display.
  • The camera module includes a privacy shutter.
  • The display supports MAXHUB Pivot.
Setup & Add-Ons

Start With the Display & Add Accessories as Needed

Optional MAXHUB accessories plug into dedicated ports, so a space can add control, audio or sharing later.

Slot-In Room PC

Windows 11 IoT runs on an Intel Core i5-1240P with TPM 2.0.

TCP33T Touch Console

This optional console connects to a dedicated PC port.

UC BM45 Speakerphones

Up to three units link through the display’s Mic In (RJ45) port.

WT13M & WT13S Dongles

Pair one through USB-A for wireless content sharing.

Enable Ports in MAXHUB Align

The display’s HDMI inputs and USB-B touch port are switched off by default, and they can be turned on in the MAXHUB Align app.

FAQs

Common Questions on XBoard V7 MTR

01Which screen sizes are available?

There are five models. The V555T (55″), V655T (65″), V755T (75″) and V865T (86″) have 4K 16:9 screens, and the V925T (92″) has a 5K 21:9 screen at 5120 × 2160. Panel type and brightness differ by model, so check the comparison table before choosing.

02Is the XBoard V7 MTR certified for Microsoft Teams Rooms?

It is a certified Microsoft Teams Rooms device, and Teams Rooms runs on its slot-in PC with Windows 11 IoT.

03Can visitors connect their own laptop?

BYOD mode allows this. After the USB-B touch port is enabled in the MAXHUB Align app, a connected laptop can use the display’s cameras, microphones and touch.

04How is content shared during a meeting?

In Teams Rooms mode, the HDMI input on the PC module shares wired content at up to 4K on the room display. Wireless sharing works through a WT13M or WT13S dongle or the MAXHUB Share app.

05What pickup range do the microphones cover?

The 16-mic array covers 180°, with a best pickup distance of 8m and a maximum of 15m. Up to three UC BM45 expansion speakerphones can be added through the Mic In port.

06Do the cameras follow the person speaking?

Yes. Speaker tracking and multi-speaker tracking work with auto framing and intelligent focus, and the camera module has a privacy shutter.

07Does the box include the TCP33T touch console?

No. The TCP33T is optional and connects to a dedicated RJ45 port on the PC module. The box contains the wireless module, a 3m HDMI cable, a power cord, two passive styluses and a wall mount kit.

08Where can the display be mounted?

It can go on a wall with the included mount kit. The VESA pattern is 400 × 200mm on the V555T, 600 × 400mm on the V655T, 800 × 400mm on the V755T, and 800 × 600mm for the V865T and V925T. The ST33M and ST23G mobile stands are available as options.

09For how many hours a day can it run?

Its rated operation is 16 hours a day, 7 days a week, and the panel life is more than 50,000 hours.

10Under what warranty is the XBoard V7 MTR covered?

All five models come with a 3-year warranty. Please check with GS IT for the terms that apply to your project.
