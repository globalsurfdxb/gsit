Features

One Wall for Chalk, Pens & Digital Lessons

The B5 Series places a 4K touch screen between writing sideboards, giving teachers analogue and digital space side by side.

Writing Synchronization

Content written on the sideboards is recorded in real time.

Chalk & Liquid Pens

The board supports writing with ordinary chalk, dustless chalk and liquid pens.

TÜV Eye Care

Low Blue Light and Flicker Free certification are held for the screen.

150° Wide-Angle Camera

A 50MP camera with a 150° diagonal view captures more of the classroom.

SkyWave 2.2 Audio

Four speakers peak at 2 × 12W + 2 × 30W and produce 88 dB at a distance of 1m.

Windows 11 Pro Inside

Inside the MT82S module, a 13th Gen Core i5 processor handles Windows 11 Pro.

Overview

Board & Screen Working as One

The MT82S PC module powers the Windows edition of MAXHUB EDU OS, which brings a wide set of education-focused applications to the board. Touch return works with Windows, Android, Linux, macOS and Chrome devices connected to the screen.

  • The surface uses Etched-AG technology with a 25% haze, non-glare finish.
  • Sideboards are offered in a black or a white finish.
  • Eight microphones pick up sound across 180° and up to 12m away.
  • Standby power stays at or below 0.5W, and the board is Energy Star certified.
  • Person counting and random election are available through the camera.
Sideboards

Write by Hand, Keep It on Record

Notes made on the sideboards can be captured as you write, and the boards come in two finishes to suit the room.

Sideboard Size

Each board measures 1115.5 × 1201.9 × 113.4mm.

Assembled Width

Screen and boards together span 4203.7mm.

Black or White Finish

Choose the sideboard colour that suits the classroom.

Wall Fixing Kit

The kit has a WIB9060K panel, strips and connectors.

Writing Tools in the Box

Two SW30 styluses and an eraser are included with the board.

FAQs

Common Questions on B5 Series

01What is the difference between the black and white versions?

Both versions share the same specifications. The only difference is the colour of the sideboards.

02What can teachers write with?

Chalk, dustless chalk and liquid pens can all be used for writing. Two SW30 styluses are also included for the touch screen.

03How does writing synchronization work?

Sideboard writing synchronization is part of MAXHUB Think&Ink. Notes written on the boards are captured in real time.

04How wide is the full board?

Once assembled, the main screen and sideboards measure 4203.7mm wide, 1214.7mm high and 118.3mm deep.

05Which computer runs the board?

An MT82S PC module powers the board, pairing a 13th Gen Core i5 processor with 8GB memory and 256GB storage under Windows 11 Pro.

06Can remote students join a lesson?

Yes. A 50MP camera with a 150° diagonal view and an 8-mic array with a 12m pickup range support remote and hybrid learning.

07How is it fixed to the wall?

Fixing hardware in the box covers a WIB9060K wall mount panel, six wall connectors and a pair of mounting strips. The board does not use VESA mounting, and the depth with the wall mount is 119mm.

08How much power does it use?

Typical consumption is 150W, standby is 0.5W or less, and the board has Energy Star certification.
