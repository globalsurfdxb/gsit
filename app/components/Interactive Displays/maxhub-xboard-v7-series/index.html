Features

Clear Video, Sound & Sharing

Cameras, audio, touch and sharing tools are combined in one display for video calls and group work.

Trident Lens System

A 50MP wide camera and two 50MP telephoto cameras give a 99° diagonal view and 5x hybrid zoom.

Presets & Speaker Tracking

Presets, auto framing, speaker tracking and intelligent focus help the camera follow the discussion.

15m Voice Pickup

Sixteen microphones cover a 180° angle and pick up speech at distances of up to 15m.

Wireless Screen Sharing

Guests can share through MAXHUB Share, a dongle, Miracast, AirPlay or Chromecast.

Windows Ink Writing

Handwriting recognition, palm rejection and sharp erase work with the 50-point infrared touch layer.

Accurate 4K Colour

Colours are rated at Delta E ≤ 1.5 on a non-glare screen with 25% haze.

Overview

Ready for the Rooms Where Your Teams Meet Daily

The XBoard V7 Series runs MAXHUB OS 7.0 powered by Windows on a slot-in PC module, with the MT71E and MT71F available as module options. A meeting launcher, a whiteboard and MAXHUB Share for wireless content are part of the system.

  • Four sizes are offered: 55, 65, 75 and 86 inches, each at 3840 × 2160.
  • Ambient light and ultrasonic detection sensors are fitted to all four models.
  • A privacy shutter is fitted to the camera module.
  • With a PC module fitted, Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.2 are available.
  • The range meets IP5X design standards and carries Energy Star certification.
Management & Integration

Central Administration & Control System Support

IT teams can manage the displays centrally, and room control systems can operate them over standard connections.

MAXHUB Pivot

Device management is handled through MAXHUB Pivot.

Microsoft Intune

Intune support depends on the PC module.

RS232 & IP Control

Room controllers can send commands over RS232 or IP.

Crestron, Extron & Q-SYS

Integration with these control platforms is supported.

Networked Microphone Input

The display’s RJ45 input accepts microphones and supports 30W PoE, microphone cascading and the AES67 protocol.

FAQs

Common Questions on XBoard V7 Series

01How is the XBoard V7 Series different from the XBoard V7 MTR?

The XBoard V7 Series runs MAXHUB OS 7.0 powered by Windows, with the MT71E and MT71F PC modules offered as options. The XBoard V7 MTR carries Microsoft Teams Rooms certification and its own slot-in computer uses Windows 11 IoT.

02Which sizes and panels are offered?

Four 4K models are available: the V5550 (55″, ADS), V6550 (65″, VA), V7550 (75″, VA) and V8650 (86″, IPS). All four are rated at 350 nits typical brightness.

03Does the display come with a PC module?

The MT71E and MT71F pluggable PC modules are optional, with Windows 10 IoT or Windows 11 Pro as the module system. Please confirm the configuration you need when you contact GS IT.

04How do guests share their screens?

Guests can use the MAXHUB Share app or a wireless dongle, and Miracast, AirPlay, Chromecast and P2P are supported. The USB-C input also accepts DP 1.2 video and supports BYOD.

05Can it work with our room control system?

RS232 and IP control are both available. Integration with Crestron, Extron and Q-SYS control platforms is also supported.

06How far do the microphones reach?

Sixteen microphones pick up speech across 180° at up to 15m. Processing includes an intelligent audio fence, AI noise cancellation and echo cancellation.

07What is included with each display?

Each model includes a 3m power cord, a 1.5m HDMI cable, two styluses and an SI07B wireless adapter. The wall mount is the WIB6540A on the V5550 and the WIB9060H on the other three sizes.

08What warranty is included?

The XBoard V7 Series comes with a 3-year warranty. Our team can confirm the cover for your installation.
