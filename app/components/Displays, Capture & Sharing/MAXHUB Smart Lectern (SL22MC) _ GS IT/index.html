Features

Write, Share & Speak From One Place

Presenters can run a complete session from the lectern with no extra equipment on stage.

Laminated Touch Screen

The 21.5″ full HD screen uses full lamination and capacitive touch with palm rejection.

Wireless Screen Sharing

Laptops and mobile devices can share to the lectern, with up to eight connected at once.

Electric Height Control

Buttons raise or lower the lectern between 1.0m and 1.2m at 0.9cm per second.

Gooseneck Microphones

Two microphones pick up the presenter at distances of up to 0.3m.

Windows PC Inside

An OPS72C module with 16GB of RAM and 256GB of storage runs Windows 10 IoT Enterprise.

Several Audio Outputs

XLR, 6.5mm and 3.5mm outputs connect to a room sound system.

Overview

Present & Write While Facing the Audience

The SL22MC combines the screen, computer and microphones in a single lectern. Two casters let staff move it between rooms.

  • Dual-band Wi-Fi connects on 2.4GHz and 5GHz networks.
  • An HDMI output sends the lectern screen to an LED wall or display.
  • Two side USB 2.0 ports accept presenter drives and devices.
  • A gigabit RJ45 port links the lectern to the wired network.
  • Physical buttons control power, height and microphone mute.
Screen Sharing

From Any Device to the Main Display

Content can move from a presenter device to the lectern and then onto the main screen.

Device to Lectern

Windows, macOS, iOS and Android devices share through the MAXHUB Share app.

Lectern to Display

The lectern screen reaches the main display over HDMI or a wireless connection.

Notes on Shared Content

Presenters can annotate a shared device screen while it is relayed to the display.

Quick Switching

Up to eight devices stay connected, and the presenter switches between them instantly.

MAXHUB LED Walls

On the same WLAN, a MAXHUB integrated LED wall receives the lectern screen with no extra apps.

FAQs

Common Questions on Smart Lectern (SL22MC)

01What computer is built into the SL22MC?

It uses an OPS72C module with an Intel Core i5-1240P, 16GB of RAM and 256GB of storage, running Windows 10 IoT Enterprise.

02How does the height adjustment work?

Two buttons drive an electric lift that moves the top between 1.0m and 1.2m at 0.9cm per second.

03Which devices can share to the lectern?

Devices running Windows 7, macOS 10.10, iOS 9.0, Android 5.0 or later can share once the MAXHUB Share app is installed.

04How many devices can connect at the same time?

Up to eight devices can stay connected, and the presenter can switch between them instantly.

05Which displays can it connect to?

It works with MAXHUB LED walls and commercial displays, and any display with an HDMI input can take the lectern screen by cable.

06Can it connect to a room sound system?

Yes. It has XLR, 6.5mm and 3.5mm audio outputs for amplifiers and mixers.

07Can the lectern be moved between spaces?

Yes. Two casters let it be rolled into position, and it weighs 29.9kg.

08What warranty applies to the lectern?

The SL22MC carries a 3-year warranty.
