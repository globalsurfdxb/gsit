Features

Slim, Bright & Easy to Run

Rooms get a large, bright picture from a single slim unit that is simple to install and maintain.

COB or SMD LEDs

On COB models, flip chip LEDs and an optical film add protection and reduce moiré.

3840Hz Refresh

The 3840Hz refresh rate and 13-bit grey scale give steady, detailed images.

31mm All-in-One Design

Each cabinet weighs 3.3kg, and the whole display is 31mm thick.

Scene Modes

Preset scene modes adapt the display to different uses.

Wireless Sharing

Built-in screen sharing works over Wi-Fi 6, with Bluetooth 5.3 also supported.

Central Control

An RS232 port allows the display to be managed by a central control system.

Overview

Fewer Parts to Install & Maintain

The display has an octa-core processor with 4GB of RAM and 64GB of storage running Android 13. It can be wall-mounted, ceiling-mounted or placed on an optional stand.

  • Two HDMI inputs (HDMI 2.0 and HDMI 1.4) accept external sources.
  • Brightness and colour temperature can both be adjusted.
  • Settings can be changed over Wi-Fi, a network cable or USB.
  • Standby power stays at 0.5W or less.
  • Built-in speakers emit sound through the gaps in the frame.
Installation

Mounting Choices & Front-Access Service

The display can be mounted in several ways and serviced from the front.

Wall Mounting

Wall mounting uses the supplied mounting bar.

Ceiling Mounting

Ceiling installation is also supported.

Optional Stand

An optional floor stand is available.

Front Service

Modules are removed from the front and can be hot-swapped.

In the Box

A remote control, a front maintenance tool, a tool kit and a wall-mounting bar are included.

FAQs

Common Questions on Raptor Series V3 Lite

01What is the difference between the COB and SMD models?

The COB models (LP135V07C and LP162V12C) use flip chip COB LEDs with an optical film, 15000:1 contrast and 170° viewing angles. The SMD models (LP135F07 and LP162F07) have 6500:1 contrast and 160° viewing angles.

02Which sizes and resolutions are available?

The 135″ models run at 1920 × 1080. At 162″, the LP162F07 runs at 1920 × 1080 and the LP162V12C at 2304 × 1296.

03Does it need a separate controller?

No. The processor, control and power distribution are built into the display, so no external controller or power cabinet is needed.

04How can it be installed?

It can be wall-mounted with the supplied bar, ceiling-mounted or placed on an optional stand.

05How is the display serviced?

Modules are accessed from the front and can be hot-swapped with the supplied maintenance tool.

06What viewing distance is recommended?

A viewing distance of 3.2m or more is recommended.

07Can it be used in direct sunlight?

It is an indoor product and should be kept out of direct sunlight.

08How is content shared to the screen?

Built-in wireless screen sharing works over Wi-Fi 6, and two HDMI inputs connect wired sources.
