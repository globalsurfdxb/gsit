Features

Record, Stream & Share Each Lesson

Schools can record and stream teaching sessions with a system that runs from one touch screen.

AI Tracking PTZ Camera

The EP30 shoots in 4K and follows the presenter by recognising clothing, body shape, hairstyle and face.

Dual-Lens Student Camera

The EW30S pairs a 110° panoramic lens with a 42° close-up lens and tracks students without extra hardware.

Ceiling Microphone Array

Eight microphones in the CM30 cover a pickup radius of 8m or more.

Wireless Lavalier Microphones

Both LM50 microphones can connect at once and be worn on a collar, around the neck or on a headset.

Seven Recording Channels

The host captures the main view, courseware, board and teacher and student views together.

Live Streaming

Up to three RTMP live streams run at 1080p while the lesson records.

Overview

Touch Control for Recording & Live Video

The CSP30 host shows the recording on its 15.6″ touch screen as it happens. It recognises connected cameras and microphones without manual matching.

  • Recordings are saved as MP4 files at 60, 30 or 25fps.
  • A 1TB drive inside the host stores recorded sessions.
  • Three HDMI outputs send video to displays in the room.
  • MAXHUB Caster supports remote observation of teaching.
  • PoE is available on three of the host network ports.
How the System Connects

Cameras & Mics, One Capture Workflow

Cameras and microphones feed the capture host, which records, displays and streams the combined video.

CSP30 Capture Host

The 15.6″ touch host runs Linux on an eight-core processor with 8GB of RAM and a 1TB drive.

In its box
  • Power adapter, power cable and audio cable
  • Five external antennas and an antenna adapter cable
  • Wall mount bracket, cable cover and screws

EP30 PTZ Camera

This 4K camera has HDMI, USB 3.0 and network outputs, and runs on 12V DC or PoE.

In its box
  • Wall mount bracket
  • Bracket installation parts
  • Quick start guide

EW30S Student Camera

The dual-lens camera turns 45° each way and connects over USB-C or a PoE network port.

In its box
  • Wall mount bracket
  • Bracket installation parts
  • Quick start guide

CM30 Ceiling Microphone

Several CM30 units can be cascaded over Cat5e cable to cover larger rooms.

In its box
  • Two suspension rod brackets
  • Two sets of bracket installation parts
  • Quick start guide

LM50 Wireless Microphone

Two clip-on microphones charge in their case and pair automatically when they are taken out.

In its box
  • USB-C charging cable
  • Three wind covers
  • Lanyard, storage bag and headset

Platforms & Streaming

Teams, Zoom and Google Meet are supported, and live streams can go to platforms such as YouTube.

FAQs

Common Questions on Capture System V3 (4K)

01Which devices make up the Capture System V3?

It includes the CSP30 capture host, the EP30 PTZ camera, the EW30S student camera, the CM30 ceiling microphone and the LM50 wireless lavalier microphone.

02What resolution does it record at?

The host records at up to 4K at 30fps, as well as 1080p, 720p, VGA and QVGA, and saves files as MP4.

03How many views can be recorded at once?

Up to seven: the main view, courseware, board view, teacher panoramic and close-up views, and student panoramic and close-up views.

04Can lessons be streamed live?

Yes. The host supports up to three RTMP live streams at 1080p, and platforms such as YouTube can receive them.

05How does the PTZ camera follow the teacher?

The EP30 uses AI re-identification to track a person by their clothing, body shape, hairstyle and facial features.

06How are the cameras powered?

Both cameras run on 12V DC or PoE (802.3af), and the capture host has three PoE network ports.

07Can the ceiling microphone cover a large room?

One CM30 covers a pickup radius of 8m or more, and several units can be cascaded over Cat5e cable.

08How long does the wireless microphone last?

The LM50 runs for more than 6 hours per charge and fully recharges in 1 hour.
