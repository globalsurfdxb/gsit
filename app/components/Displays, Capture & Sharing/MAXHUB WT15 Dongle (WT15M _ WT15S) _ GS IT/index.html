Features

Share in 4K, Straight From the Laptop

Presenters can put their screen on the room display quickly, with no cables across the table.

4K Screen Sharing

Content is shared at up to 3840 × 2160 at 30Hz for clear slides and video.

Driver-Free on PC

The dongle works as soon as it is plugged in, with no software to install.

Multi-Screen Sharing

Several participants can cast at the same time or in a split-screen layout.

Wireless Touchback

People can control their laptop directly from an interactive display.

Wireless BYOD

On the WT15M, a laptop can use the room camera, microphone and speakers wirelessly.

Secure Connection

AES 128-bit encryption, dynamic key negotiation and multi-factor authentication protect each link.

Overview

Cable-Free Meeting Tables

The WT15 plugs into a USB-C port, and a USB-A adapter covers laptops without one. It connects over 5GHz Wi-Fi from up to 12m away.

  • Windows 7, 10 and 11 and macOS 10.12 or later are supported.
  • Power use stays at 2.5W or less.
  • The cable has passed over 20,000 bending tests.
  • A lanyard hole helps keep the dongle from going missing.
  • Each dongle weighs 73g.
Indicator Light

Clear Status at Each Step

The light on the dongle shows what it is doing, so presenters know when they are ready to share.

Starting Up

A flashing white light shows the dongle is starting.

Pairing With the Room

Blue flashes while pairing and turns solid once paired, and red means pairing failed.

Pairing With the Laptop

White flashes during pairing and stays solid when the laptop is paired.

Sharing the Screen

A solid green light confirms the screen is being shared.

Firmware Updates

A flashing blue light also appears while the dongle is updating.

FAQs

Common Questions on WT15 Dongle

01What is the difference between the WT15M and WT15S?

The WT15M supports wireless BYOD, so a laptop can use the room camera, microphone and speakers. The WT15S handles screen sharing and touchback only.

02Does the dongle need software or drivers?

No. It is plug-and-play on a PC, with no software installation.

03Which operating systems are supported?

Windows 7, 10 and 11, and macOS 10.12 or later.

04How far can the laptop be from the room system?

The wireless connection reaches up to 12m (39ft).

05Can more than one person share at a time?

Yes. Several participants can cast at the same time or use a split-screen layout.

06Can the laptop be controlled from the display?

Yes. Touchback lets people control their laptop directly from an interactive display.

07Which laptop ports does it fit?

It plugs into USB-C and works with a USB-A adapter on laptops that have USB-A ports.

08How is the wireless connection secured?

It uses AES 128-bit multi-layered encryption, dynamic key negotiation and multi-factor authentication.
