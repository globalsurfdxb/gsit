Features

Display, Share & Manage With Ease

Content stays clear and easy to read in busy rooms and public areas throughout the day.

4K Anti-Glare Screen

A 3840 × 2160 panel with 500-nit brightness and 25% haze stays readable under room lighting.

Accurate Colour

98% sRGB coverage, 10-bit colour and factory calibration keep images true to the source.

24/7 Operation

The displays are rated for continuous use in landscape or portrait orientation.

Wireless Sharing

MAXHUB Share works with BYOD, Miracast, AirPlay and Chromecast connections.

Room System Control

RS232, IP control and HDMI CEC work with Crestron, Q-SYS and Extron control systems.

Remote Management

Device control and content scheduling are handled remotely in MAXHUB Pivot.

Overview

One Range for Many Spaces

The CMB Series runs Android 14 with MAXHUB Signage OS V2 on a quad-core processor with a 1.5 TOPS AI unit. It works with more than 20 third-party content management tools, including Appspace.

  • RJ45 in and out ports run at up to 1000Mbps.
  • Three HDMI 2.0 inputs accept 4K at 60Hz, with CEC and ARC on HDMI 1.
  • The USB-C port provides 65W of charging to a connected laptop.
  • Wake on LAN, play via URL and auto input change are supported.
  • IP5X dust protection and Energy Star apply across the range.
Installation

Ready to Mount, With the Right Bracket

Each size ships with its own wall mount bracket and display brackets.

Wall Mount Included

Each display ships with a matching wall mount bracket.

Slim Wall Depth

Wall depth is 101mm or less with the supplied bracket.

Optional XBar Bracket

The 65″–86″ models accept an optional XBar Series Bracket.

Cross-Brand Mounting

Other video bars mount with the optional WIB20 bracket.

In the Box

A 3m power cord, a 3m HDMI cable, a remote control, two display brackets, a wall mount bracket and a Wi-Fi module are included.

FAQs

Common Questions on CMB Series

01Which sizes are in the CMB Series?

The range covers 43″, 50″, 55″, 65″, 75″, 86″ and 98″ models, ND43CMB through ND98CMB.

02Can the displays run around the clock?

Yes. Each model is rated for 24/7 operation and can be installed in landscape or portrait orientation.

03How bright are the screens?

Typical brightness is 500 nits, and a 25% haze surface reduces glare.

04Which operating system do they use?

They run Android 14 with MAXHUB Signage OS V2 on a quad-core A73 processor with 4GB of RAM and 32GB of storage.

05Can laptops share content to the screen?

MAXHUB Share supports BYOD, Miracast, AirPlay and Chromecast. The USB-C port also carries video, audio and 65W charging.

06Do they work with room control systems?

RS232 and IP control are supported, with compatibility for Crestron, Q-SYS and Extron.

07How does the 50″ model differ from the others?

The ND50CMB uses a VA panel with 5,000:1 typical contrast and a 6.5ms response time.

08What warranty applies to the CMB Series?

Each display comes with a 3-year warranty. Please check with GS IT for the terms that apply to your project.
