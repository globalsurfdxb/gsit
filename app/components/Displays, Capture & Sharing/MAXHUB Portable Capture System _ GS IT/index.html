Features

Quick to Deploy, Wireless by Design

Events can be filmed and streamed in places that have no fixed AV installation.

Wireless PTZ Camera

The EP50 combines 20x optical and 40x hybrid zoom to bring distant subjects into clear view.

AI Speaker Tracking

The camera keeps the presenter in frame automatically, and the operator can click to choose the target.

Touch Directing

Cameras and scenes are switched from the 15.6″ touch screen on the CSP50M host.

Dual Networking

Wi-Fi 6 and a wired connection can back each other up during an event.

Wireless Microphones

LM50 microphones connect on their own when removed from the charging case.

Battery Powered

The EP50 runs for up to 6 hours on its built-in battery, and the microphones charge in their case.

Overview

Events, Lectures & Outdoor Activities

The host, wireless camera and microphones work together without fixed cabling. Each part can be placed where the event needs it.

  • An array microphone in the camera picks up sound within 5m.
  • A screen on the camera shows its battery status.
  • A 512GB SSD in the host stores recordings as MP4 files.
  • The host connects to a MAXHUB whiteboard automatically.
  • With its screen lowered, the host is 70mm deep.
How the System Connects

Camera & Mics, Linked Over the Air

The camera and microphones send video and audio to the host, which handles directing and live output.

CSP50M Capture Host

The host records up to seven channels and streams to three RTMP destinations at 1080p.

In its box
  • Power adapter and power cable
  • Five external antennas and an antenna adapter cable
  • Two Phoenix connectors

EP50 Wireless PTZ Camera

This 4K camera pans 170° each way and has HDMI, USB-C and network outputs.

In its box
  • Power adapter
  • Power cable
  • Quick start guide

LM50 Wireless Microphone

Two clip-on microphones charge in their case and pair automatically when they are taken out.

In its box
  • USB-C charging cable
  • Three wind covers
  • Lanyard, storage bag and headset

Suitable Venues

The system can be used at outdoor events, in theatres and in large halls.

FAQs

Common Questions on Portable Capture System

01What does the Portable Capture System include?

It combines the CSP50M portable capture host, the EP50 wireless PTZ camera and LM50 wireless lavalier microphones.

02Does the camera need a power outlet?

No. The EP50 has a built-in battery, so it can be placed away from power sources. A power adapter is also included.

03How long do the batteries last?

The EP50 camera films for up to 6 hours per charge. The LM50 microphone runs for more than 6 hours and recharges fully in 1 hour.

04How far can the camera be from the host?

Wireless audio and video transmission reaches up to 100m.

05Can the camera track a presenter?

Yes. The EP50 follows the presenter automatically, and the operator can click to select the target.

06How does the system connect to the network?

The CSP50M supports Wi-Fi 6 on 2.4GHz, 5GHz and 5.8GHz and has RJ45 ports for wired networking.

07How much can the host record?

The CSP50M records up to seven channels at up to 4K at 30fps and stores MP4 files on a 512GB SSD.

08How do the microphones connect?

The LM50 microphones begin pairing each time they are removed from the charging case. Pairing is required the first time they are used.
