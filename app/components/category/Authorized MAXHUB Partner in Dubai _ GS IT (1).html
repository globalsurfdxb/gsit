FAQs

Questions About MAXHUB Projects

01How do I choose the right display size?

Match the size to the space and the furthest viewing distance. MAXHUB interactive displays range from 55 to 86 inches, with a 92-inch 21:9 Teams Rooms model, and Raptor LED walls go up to 220 inches for halls and auditoriums. We confirm the size after measuring the space during the site survey.

02How is an interactive display different from a commercial display?

An interactive display such as the XBoard V7 or F5 Series has a touch screen for writing and annotation, and many models add cameras and microphones. A commercial display such as the CMB Series is a 4K screen for signage and shared content, rated for 24/7 use. Some interactive models need a slot-in PC module to run Windows.

03Which calling and teaching platforms does MAXHUB support?

It depends on the model. XBoard for Teams Rooms, the XBar W70, V70 and V50 kits, the TCP33T console and the UC BM45 are certified for Microsoft Teams Rooms, other models run Zoom, Google Meet or Webex, and the U4 Series is Google EDLA certified. We confirm platform support for each model in your proposal.

04Can people share content without installing software?

In many cases, yes. MAXHUB Share accepts AirPlay, Miracast and Chromecast on supported displays, and MAXHUB Connect shares from a browser link with no client to install. A WT15 dongle is another option for laptops.

05Which audio option suits my space?

Choose by the size of the space and how people speak in it. The UC BM35 covers up to 6 m and the UC BM45 up to 8 m, with more BM45 units linked for longer tables. In large classrooms, the Voice Lift System carries the teacher's voice through ceiling speakers.

06Is GS IT an Authorized MAXHUB Partner?

Yes. GS IT is an Authorized MAXHUB Partner based in Dubai, and we supply, install and configure MAXHUB collaboration technology for businesses, schools and universities across the UAE.

07Who handles warranty claims for MAXHUB equipment?

GS IT does, for as long as the units are under manufacturer warranty. We raise the claim with MAXHUB and arrange the replacement. Coverage differs between products, and your proposal states the terms for each item.

08Do I need an Annual Maintenance Contract?

No, the Annual Maintenance Contract is optional. With an AMC, you get firmware updates, help with user queries and fault resolution, plus a 15-minute response on critical issues. Our standard service still tracks warranty and licence dates, sends reminders and handles renewals.
