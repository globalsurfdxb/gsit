FAQs

FortiManager questions answered

How many FortiGates make FortiManager worthwhile?

Once you manage more than a few FortiGates, or need the same policy across sites, central management saves time and avoids mismatched configuration. For one or two small sites, FortiGate Cloud is often enough.

Cloud or VM?

FortiManager Cloud needs no servers and starts at 3 devices. A VM suits larger estates or organisations that must keep management data in their own environment.

How are HA pairs counted?

On FortiManager VM and appliances, the secondary unit of an HA pair is not counted. On FortiManager Cloud, each FortiGate in the cluster needs to be covered by the subscription.

Can it manage our switches and access points?

Yes. FortiManager manages FortiSwitch and FortiAP through the FortiGate that controls them.

Will importing our FortiGates change their configuration?

No. Devices are imported with their current configuration. We then move them to shared policy packages in stages, with your approval at each step.

Do you deploy and support it?

Yes. GS IT is a Fortinet Authorized Partner. We deploy FortiManager, onboard your devices, build policy packages and support it after go-live. Under a GS IT AMC contract, critical issues get a 15-minute response.
