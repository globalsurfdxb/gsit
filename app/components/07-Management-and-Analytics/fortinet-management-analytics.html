How it fits together

Configure, log, investigate, improve

FortiManager and FortiAnalyzer share the same device list, so changes and their effects are visible in one loop.

  1. Configure

    FortiManager pushes policies and firmware to every FortiGate.

  2. Collect

    Every device sends its logs to FortiAnalyzer or FortiGate Cloud.

  3. Detect

    Outbreak alerts and event handlers flag what needs attention.

  4. Investigate

    FortiAI summarises the incident and suggests next steps.

  5. Fix

    The change goes out through FortiManager, with an audit trail.

FAQs

Management and analytics questions answered

Do we need FortiManager and FortiAnalyzer, or is FortiGate Cloud enough?

For a few small sites, FortiGate Cloud covers management, reports and one year of logs per FortiGate. As you add sites, VDOMs or compliance needs, FortiManager and FortiAnalyzer give you deeper policy control, longer retention options and SOC features.

Cloud or on-premises?

Both run the same software. FortiManager Cloud and FortiAnalyzer Cloud need no servers and are licensed per device. A VM or appliance suits organisations that must keep logs inside the UAE or need very large log volumes.

How is FortiManager licensed?

By the number of managed devices. One FortiGate counts as one device, or one per VDOM when VDOMs are enabled. The secondary unit of an HA pair is not counted on the VM and appliance models.

How is FortiAnalyzer licensed?

By log volume in GB per day. For FortiAnalyzer Cloud, each FortiGate subscription includes a daily allowance based on its size, and you add 5, 50 or 500 GB per day if you need more.

How long are logs kept?

FortiGate Cloud keeps one year of logs with a subscription. FortiAnalyzer retention depends on the storage you license, and we size it to your audit and compliance needs.

What does FortiAI add?

A plain-language assistant inside FortiManager and FortiAnalyzer. It can summarise incidents, write queries and reports, and help with configuration, so your team spends less time on routine work.

Do you set it up and run it?

Yes. GS IT is a Fortinet Authorized Partner. We deploy FortiManager and FortiAnalyzer, onboard your devices, build policy packages and reports, and can monitor them for you. Under a GS IT AMC contract, critical issues get a 15-minute response.
