Overview

A Certified Console for Flexible Room Setups

The TCP33T works with Surface Hub and the MAXHUB XBoard V7 Teams Room. It connects to the XBoard with a single RJ45 cable, and to a Surface Hub through the included PoE adapter and an interface converter.

  • The console is certified for Microsoft Teams Rooms.
  • The display is rated for 20,000 hours or more of use.
  • Screen brightness reaches up to 300 nits through the glass.
  • A Kensington slot allows the console to be secured with a lock.
  • Operating conditions cover 0 to 40 °C and 10% to 90% humidity.

FAQs

Common Questions on TCP33T Universal Console

01Which systems does the TCP33T work with?

It works with Surface Hub Teams Rooms and the MAXHUB XBoard V7 Teams Room.

02How does it connect to an XBoard?

One RJ45 cable runs directly between the console and the board.

03How is it connected to a Surface Hub?

It links through the included PoE adapter and UD20A interface converter.

04Can laptops share content through it?

Yes. A laptop connected to the USB-C port can share its screen in the meeting.

05Does it need a separate power supply?

It is powered over PoE, and a PoE adapter is included in the box.

06Can the console be wall mounted?

Yes. It can be placed on a table or mounted on a wall.

07How does the console wake up?

A PIR motion sensor wakes it automatically when someone approaches.

08What is included with the console?

The package holds two 7.5m Ethernet cables, a 1.8m USB-C cable, a PoE adapter, a UD20A interface converter, three power cords, a screwdriver and a quick start guide.
