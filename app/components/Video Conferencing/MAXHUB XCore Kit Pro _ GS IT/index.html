Features

Meeting Tools Housed in a Compact Mini-PC

People can start meetings, share content and control the room with ease.

One-Cable Console Link

A single Ethernet cable connects the TCP30T console to the mini-PC, which keeps installation simple.

Dual Room Displays

Two room displays can show shared content and the camera feed at the same time.

4K Wired Sharing

The HDMI input on the XC25T shares content at up to 4K resolution.

Wireless Sharing

Content can be shared in Teams Rooms with an optional MAXHUB wireless dongle or the MAXHUB Share app.

Second Console Option

Up to two consoles can control the room together, which helps in larger spaces.

Display Sleep & Wake

Room displays sleep and wake automatically with the HDMI video signal to save energy.

Overview

Start Meetings With One Touch

Windows 11 IoT Enterprise SAC and Microsoft Teams Rooms run on the XC25T, which has a 12th gen Intel Core i5-1240P, 16GB of DDR4 memory and 256GB of storage. Cameras, microphones and speakers connect to the mini-PC.

  • MAXHUB Pivot comes pre-installed for remote maintenance and updates.
  • A PIR sensor wakes the TCP30T when someone approaches.
  • Laptops share at 1080p through the console’s USB-C port.
  • Three HDMI outputs and Wi-Fi 6 are built into the mini-PC.
  • The mini-PC also has a Kensington slot for a security lock.

FAQs

Common Questions on XCore Kit Pro

01What is included in the XCore Kit Pro?

The kit includes the XC25T mini-PC and the TCP30T touch console. The mini-PC comes with a wall mount kit, two 3m HDMI cables, a power adapter and a power cable, and the console comes with a 1.8m USB-C cable and a 7.5m Ethernet cable.

02Does the kit include a camera or speakerphone?

No. The UC P30 PTZ camera and the UC BM45 table speakerphone are optional accessories, and cameras, microphones and speakers connect to the mini-PC.

03Is it certified for Microsoft Teams Rooms?

Yes. It is a Microsoft Teams certified kit, and Teams Rooms runs on Windows 11 IoT Enterprise SAC on the XC25T.

04How many displays can it drive?

It supports dual room displays, so content and the camera stream can be viewed together. The mini-PC has three HDMI outputs.

05How can people share content?

The HDMI input on the mini-PC shares at up to 4K, and the console’s USB-C port shares a laptop at 1080p. Wireless sharing works with an optional WT13M or WT13S dongle or the MAXHUB Share app.

06Can a second console be added?

Yes. The TCP30T has an RJ45 port for an optional second console, and up to two consoles can control the room at once.

07How is the kit managed remotely?

MAXHUB Pivot comes pre-installed, so IT managers can maintain and update the kit without visiting the room.

08What warranty does the XCore Kit Pro come with?

It comes with a standard 3-year warranty. Our team can confirm the terms for your project.
