Features

Wide Coverage, Close Detail on Demand

Remote participants get a well-framed view of whoever is speaking, wherever they are seated.

Dual-Lens Design

12x Optical Zoom

Overview

Sharp Faces Across the Room

The UC P30 pairs a PTZ camera with an 8MP panoramic camera, so the wide lens finds participants while the PTZ lens frames them. It can be managed remotely in MAXHUB Pivot, set up over USB in MAXHUB Align, or controlled from MAXHUB Teams Rooms products.

  • Video reaches 4K at 30fps over USB 3.0 and IP.
  • A security lock slot helps protect the camera in shared spaces.
  • The housing is made from high-strength aluminium alloy with an anodised finish.
  • Image flip and mirror settings suit inverted mounting positions.
  • Pro AV integration covers MAXHUB displays, Extron controls and Shure audio.
Connection Options

Connect by USB, HDMI or the Network

The UC P30 connects over USB, HDMI or IP, so it can be used with a PC, a display or an IP video system.

USB 3.0 Type-C

H.264 and MJPEG reach 2160p at 30fps over USB 3.0.

HDMI 1.4 Output

One HDMI 1.4 output provides a wired video feed.

IP Streaming & PoE+

The RJ45 port carries two IP streams and PoE+ power.

RS232 Control

VISCA, Pelco-D and Pelco-P protocols are supported.

Single-Cable Installation

With PoE+, one Cat5e or Cat6 cable supplies power and IP streaming.

FAQs

Common Questions on UC P30

01What rooms is the UC P30 designed for?

It is designed for medium and large meeting rooms.

02How do the two cameras work together?

The 120° panoramic camera detects participants in real time and directs the PTZ camera to frame the best view.

03How much zoom does it offer?

The PTZ camera has 12x optical zoom with a 4.8–57.8mm lens.

04Which connections are available?

Video can be sent over USB 3.0 Type-C, HDMI 1.4 or IP through the RJ45 port, and an RS232 port supports VISCA, Pelco-D and Pelco-P control.

05Can the camera run on PoE?

Yes. It accepts PoE+ up to 25W or a 12V 2A DC supply, and it uses 22W or less.

06How is privacy handled?

When the camera is not in use, the PTZ camera tilts downward and the electric privacy cover remains closed.

07Which software manages the camera?

MAXHUB Pivot handles remote management, MAXHUB Align handles on-site setup over USB, and MAXHUB Teams Rooms products offer room control.

08Can the camera be mounted upside down?

Image flip and mirror settings correct the picture when the camera is mounted upside down.
