Features

Sharp Video For Small & Medium Rooms

Remote participants see a detailed picture and hear natural-sounding audio.

100MP Dual Camera

Each of the two 50MP cameras has a 1/1.55 inch sensor, and video reaches 3840 × 2160.

125° Field of View

The lenses cover 130° diagonally and 125° horizontally, with 6x digital zoom for closer shots.

Framing That Follows

Auto framing and speaker tracking move the shot, and smart gathering and intelligent focus keep the group in view.

12-Mic MEMS Array

Twelve MEMS microphones are built in, and up to four expansion microphones can be added.

Noise & Echo Removal

AI noise reduction, echo cancellation and anti-reverberation cut background sounds such as keyboard clicks.

Dual 15W Speakers

Two 15W full-frequency speakers play stereo call audio with an 80Hz–20kHz response.

Overview

Bring Your Laptop, Connect With One USB Cable

The bar runs Android 13 with Microsoft Device Ecosystem Platform support and connects to Panel AP30 for touch control. A single USB cable to its USB 3.0 Type-B port connects a laptop for BYOD meetings.

  • Content shared in Teams meetings reaches 1080p at 30fps.
  • Two HDMI outputs support 4K at 60Hz with CEC.
  • Audio in and audio out ports connect third-party sound equipment.
  • Microsoft Teams Admin Center, Intune and the Pro management portal can manage the kit.
  • An electric shutter closes over the lenses automatically for privacy.
What's in the Kit

A Bar & a Touch Panel

The XBar V50 and Panel AP30 each arrive with their own power and network cables.

XBar V50 Video Bar

At 650.8mm wide and 2.8kg, the bar ships with ADA-standard mounting components.

In its box
  • Power adapter and 1.5m AC cable
  • Two 3m HDMI cables
  • 3m Ethernet cable
  • Mounting components (ADA standard)

Panel AP30 Touch Panel

A 1920 × 1080 capacitive screen gives people touch control, and its USB-C port accepts laptop content.

In its box
  • 1.5m USB-C cable
  • 7.5m Ethernet cable
  • Screwdriver
  • Power adapter and 1.5m AC cable

USB-C Power for Laptops

Panel AP30 can supply up to 65W over USB-C Power Delivery to a connected laptop.

FAQs

Common Questions on XBar V50 Kit

01Which items are in the XBar V50 Kit?

The kit holds the XBar V50 video bar and Panel AP30. The bar includes a power adapter, an AC cable, two 3m HDMI cables, a 3m Ethernet cable and ADA-standard mounting components. Panel AP30 includes a USB-C cable, a 7.5m Ethernet cable, a screwdriver, a power adapter and an AC cable.

02What size of room does it suit?

It is designed for small to medium meeting rooms, and the cameras cover 125° horizontally.

03Does it work with Microsoft Teams?

Content can be shared in Teams meetings at 1080p and 30fps. The kit can be managed in the Microsoft Teams Admin Center, Intune or the Pro management portal.

04What zoom do the cameras offer?

The two 50MP cameras offer 6x digital zoom, and AI framing and speaker tracking adjust the shot automatically.

05Can I use the bar with my own laptop?

Yes. Connect the laptop to the USB 3.0 Type-B port with a single USB cable for BYOD meetings.

06How many displays can the bar connect to?

Two HDMI outputs connect room displays at up to 4K and 60Hz, with CEC support.

07How big and heavy is the bar?

The bar measures 650.8 × 76.6 × 95.7mm and weighs 2.8kg. Packed, it measures 750 × 136 × 246mm and weighs 6.0kg.

08What operating conditions does it need?

It runs at temperatures between 0 and 40 °C with 10% to 90% relative humidity, at altitudes below 5000m.
