Overview

A Room Upgrade for Laptop-Led Meetings

XBar U50 connects to a laptop with one USB cable and acts as its wide-angle camera, full-frequency speaker and noise-reduction microphone. Setup and control settings are managed in MAXHUB Align.

  • An electric shutter closes over the lenses automatically.
  • Video and audio fences help keep the focus on the meeting area.
  • Echo cancellation and anti-reverberation reduce room echo.
  • The bar measures 650.8 × 76.6 × 95.7mm and weighs 2.68kg.
  • Maximum power draw is 85W from a 19V DC supply.

FAQs

Common Questions on XBar U50

01How does the XBar U50 connect to a computer?

It connects through its USB 3.0 Type-B port with a single cable, and a 3m USB B-A cable is included.

02Does it work with a USB 2.0 port?

Yes. The bar also works over USB 2.0 and can still send 4K video.

03Which room sizes does it suit?

The bar is intended for small to medium rooms, with a 125° horizontal view and a 12-mic array.

04Do the cameras follow whoever is talking?

Yes. Speaker tracking follows the active speaker, and auto framing, smart gathering and intelligent focus adjust the group shot.

05Can extra microphones be added?

Expansion microphones connect through the bar’s RJ45 port.

06How is the bar configured?

Control and management settings are handled in MAXHUB Align. Audio fence needs commissioning software to set up.

07What comes in the box?

The box holds a power adapter, a 1.5m AC cable, a 3m USB B-A cable, a remote control, ADA-standard mounting components and a positioning card.

08How much power does it use?

It runs on a 19V DC supply and draws up to 85W. The power adapter and AC cable are included.
